Android shows notifications one by one: they float over what you are doing and pile up in the shade. Aram takes
them out of the shade as they arrive, keeps them in its own inbox, sorted by app, and leaves one silent summary
line in the shade. What really matters still reaches you right away.

<ul>
<li><b>Inbox</b>: every app's notifications, with the sender's photo, full text and time. Tap opens the chat or email.</li>
<li><b>Copy code</b>: verification codes get a one-tap copy button.</li>
<li><b>Remind me later</b>: in 1 hour, this evening or tomorrow morning.</li>
<li><b>Reply</b> to messages right from Aram.</li>
<li><b>History</b>: the last 7, 30 or 90 days, searchable.</li>
<li><b>Stats</b>: a calendar of busy days and the week's noisiest apps.</li>
<li><b>Summary at set hours</b>, if you want it: the shade changes only at your times.</li>
<li><b>Four levels per app</b>: Important floats with a calm card, Inbox, Silent, or left in the shade.</li>
<li><b>People and words</b> that always float; <b>quiet hours</b>; no notification floods.</li>
<li>Calls, alarms, music and navigation are never touched.</li>
<li>English and Persian, light and dark. No internet permission, nothing leaves the phone.</li>
</ul>

Needs a one-time adb command (shown in the app). "Turn off and uninstall" puts the phone back exactly as it was.
